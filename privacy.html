Customs Broker License Exam · iPhone, iPad & Mac

Privacy Policy

Customs Broker License Exam does not collect any personal information. There is no account and no advertising. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (which features are used, never your answers) unless you switch them off in the Library. Apart from that, the only network traffic is Apple's: the App Store for the optional Full Access purchase and, if you leave iCloud sync on, your own iCloud account carrying your progress between your devices.

What the app collects

No personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail, and it contains no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs. The importers, sellers and brokers in its case files are fictional.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see which parts of the app are used and where people stop, the app sends a small number of anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service that stores its data in the European Union. An event is a short label such as “mock paper finished” or “Full Access screen shown”, together with the app version, the kind of device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), the language setting, whether Full Access is unlocked or was just bought, and, for a finished session, a score band (for example 60 to 79%).

Events never contain your name, email address, Apple Account, questions, answers, or exact scores. Each install is counted with a random identifier created on the device; it is hashed before it leaves the device, is not linked to you or your Apple Account, and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses.

You can turn this off at any time in Library, “Share anonymous usage statistics”. Once it is off, the app records and sends no further events.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves your study data so your progress survives between sessions. This includes:

  • which questions you have answered and whether you answered them correctly,
  • the spaced-review schedule built from those answers,
  • your mock paper scores and timings, and the case files you have checked,
  • your place in an unfinished practice session or mock paper, so you can pick up where you left off.

All of it is written to files inside the app's own storage on your device (on a Mac, inside the app's sandbox container). It is never sent to any server belonging to the developer, and the developer cannot see it. Your appearance setting stays on the device it is set on.

iCloud sync

With iCloud sync on (Book tab › Sync, on unless you turn it off), each of your devices also keeps a copy of its own progress in the private database of your iCloud account, through Apple's CloudKit, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac can combine their progress. Only devices signed in to your Apple Account can read that copy; the developer has no access to it, and it counts towards your own iCloud storage (a few tens of kilobytes per device). Turn sync off in the Book tab to keep progress on one device only. To delete the iCloud copy, use Settings › your name › iCloud › Storage › Customs Broker License Exam on an iPhone or iPad.

You can erase your progress at any time with “Reset everything” in the Book tab — with sync on, on every device signed in to your iCloud account. Deleting the app deletes the copy on that device.

Network access

Apart from the anonymous usage statistics described above, the app contacts no server belonging to the developer. The questions, explanations, the 19 CFR text, the HTSUS notes and the ACE documents are bundled inside the app, the case files and calculation drills are generated on the device, and studying works entirely offline. There is no server component.

Purchases

The app is free to try. Full Access is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store: Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether Full Access is owned. The developer never receives your name, Apple Account, payment details or any purchase data beyond Apple's standard, anonymous sales reports. The app keeps only a count of the free questions, mock papers and case files used, on your device and, with iCloud sync on, in your iCloud account, so the free allowance is shared by your devices.

Children

Customs Broker License Exam is a study aid for licensing candidates and is rated 4+. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
