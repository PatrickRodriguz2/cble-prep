Customs Broker License Exam · iPhone, iPad & Mac

Support

An independent study app for candidates preparing for the U.S. Customs Broker License Examination.

Questions, bug reports, or a question you think is wrong? Email patrguzz@gmail.com. Corrections are especially welcome: please include the question and the source you are working from (the section of 19 CFR, the HTSUS note, or the ACE document).

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.0 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26.0 or later. Studying works entirely offline; the App Store is contacted only for buying or restoring Full Access, and iCloud only to sync your progress.

Free to try, and Full Access

The app is free to try: 50 practice questions, one mock paper and one case file. Full Access is a one-time in-app purchase (no subscription) that unlocks every question, unlimited mock papers and unlimited case files on your iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account. The free allowance is shared by your synced devices.

Restoring a purchase: open the Book tab and tap “Restore purchases” in the Full Access card, or use the same button on the purchase screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How mock papers are built

A mock paper has 80 questions in 4 hours 30 minutes, sampled to the published category counts: Entry and Entry Summary, Broker Compliance, Valuation, Classification, Practical Exercise and Rotating Specialty. The practical-exercise questions come from one generated case file. The pass mark shown is 60 of 80. You can flag questions and return to them; nothing is revealed until you submit.

Case files

Each case file is generated from a number, so the same number always gives the same case. It may contain up to three planted errors or none; tap the lines you think are wrong, then check. The ledger shows what you found, what you missed and what you flagged by mistake, with the rule behind each.

Which edition does the app follow?

The book section bundles the 2024 annual edition of 19 CFR, the 2025 HTSUS general, section and chapter notes, and CBP's ACE documents, each shown with its edition. Sections amended since the 2024 edition are marked. Always check the editions named for your sitting and the current regulations before relying on anything in the app.

Progress and resetting

Your iPhone, iPad and Mac combine their progress through your own iCloud account. Sign in to iCloud on each device; the switch is in Book › Sync, and it is on unless you turn it off. Every device still keeps its own copy, so studying works offline and catches up when you are back online. To start over, use “Reset everything” in the Book tab: with sync on it erases progress on every device signed in to your iCloud account, with sync off on that device only. Deleting the app deletes the progress on that device; with sync on, the iCloud copy stays until you delete it in Settings › your name › iCloud › Storage › Customs Broker License Exam.

A question looks wrong?
